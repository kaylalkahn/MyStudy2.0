When can't you study?

Drag across the grid to block out school, practice, work and sleep. MyStudy only plans in the open hours.

  • Move a blockDrag it to another time or day. It stays pinned where you drop it.
  • Resize or splitDrag a block's top or bottom edge to change its length. Use the pencil to split it, set exact times or remove it.
  • Add a blockDrag across empty time, double-click a slot, or use + Add block.
  • Mark it doneClick a block when you finish. Click it again to undo.
  • Read the dotPressure on the task: comfortable, steady, tight, overloaded.
  • Missed a session?Unfinished hours that pass are re-planned into your next open time.
